FAQ

Your questions

Who is ConstructX for?

University students from across Pakistan are welcome.

What is included in the experience?

The event includes a Technical Workshop, Load the Bridge, Drop the Egg, a Theme Dinner and a Concert. The detailed timetable is still to be announced.

Where can I find the competition rules?

Team sizes, materials, bridge dimensions, egg drop height and scoring details will be shared with registered participants before the event. The downloadable guide introduces each module.

How do I register?

Submit the participant registration form by 25 November 2026. Enter your details exactly as they appear on your CNIC and student card. Contact the event coordinator if you need help.
