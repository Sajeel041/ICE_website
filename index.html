05 / GOOD TO KNOW

A little clarity.
A lot to look forward to.

Who is ConstructX for?+

ConstructX-4.0 brings university students from across Pakistan together through civil engineering, hands-on competition and community.

What is included in the experience?+

The event includes a Technical Workshop, Load the Bridge, Drop the Egg, a Theme Dinner and a Concert. The detailed timetable has not yet been provided.

Where can I find the competition rules?+

Team sizes, materials, bridge dimensions, egg drop height and scoring details will be shared with registered participants before the event. The downloadable guide introduces each module.

How do I register?+

Register using the ConstructX-4.0 participant registration form by 25 November 2026. Enter your details exactly as they appear on your CNIC and student card. For help, contact the event coordinator using the details below.
